# TikTok Community & Recommendation Guidelines

## Community Guidelines (Policy Violations)

### 1. Violent Extremism
- Content that promotes or glorifies violence against individuals or groups is prohibited.
- Content that supports terrorist organizations or violent extremist groups is prohibited.
- Content that threatens individuals or public figures with violence is prohibited.
- Simulated violence or threats must have clear educational, documentary, or satirical context.

### 2. Hate Speech & Hateful Behavior
- Content that attacks or dehumanizes individuals or groups based on protected attributes is prohibited: race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, disability, immigration status.
- Content promoting hateful ideologies or conspiracy theories targeting protected groups is prohibited.
- Slurs, derogatory stereotypes, and calls for exclusion or segregation are prohibited.
- Content denying well-documented historical events targeting protected groups is prohibited.

### 3. Harassment & Cyberbullying
- Targeted harassment, intimidation, or bullying of individuals is prohibited.
- Publishing private contact information (doxxing) with malicious intent is prohibited.
- Coordinated harassment campaigns against individuals are prohibited.
- Content encouraging others to harass or bully is prohibited.
- Criticism of public figures is allowed but must not cross into harassment.

### 4. Suicide, Self-Harm & Dangerous Acts
- Content that promotes, glorifies, or provides instructions for suicide or self-harm is prohibited.
- Graphic depictions of self-harm are prohibited.
- Dangerous challenges or acts that may cause serious injury or death are prohibited.
- Content about recovery or prevention with appropriate warnings may be allowed.

### 5. Child Safety & Minor Protection
- Sexualized content involving minors is strictly prohibited and reported to authorities.
- Content depicting child abuse or neglect is prohibited and reported.
- Content that puts minors at risk of harm or exploitation is prohibited.
- Minors may not appear in content involving dangerous acts, challenges, or mature themes.
- Content should not excessively feature minors in compromising situations.

### 6. Privacy & Personal Data
- Publishing others' private information without consent is prohibited: addresses, phone numbers, emails, financial information, government IDs.
- Non-consensual sharing of intimate images is prohibited.
- Recording or sharing content in private spaces without consent is prohibited.
- Content must comply with applicable privacy laws.

### 7. Spam, Fake Engagement & Deception
- Fake engagement services, bots, and automated systems to inflate metrics are prohibited.
- Spam content including repetitive, irrelevant, or misleading posts is prohibited.
- Deceptive practices to trick users into interacting are prohibited.
- Coordinated inauthentic behavior (manipulating public discussion) is prohibited.
- Impersonation of individuals, brands, or organizations with intent to deceive is prohibited.

### 8. Intellectual Property
- Content that infringes on copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights may be removed.
- Reposting content from other creators without permission or credit may be limited in recommendations.
- Using others' content as your own (plagiarism) is prohibited.
- Reporting system allows rights holders to request removal of infringing content.

### 8a. Content ID & Automated Copyright Enforcement
- Clips from movies, TV shows, professionally produced videos, or music videos are very likely to be rejected by TikTok's Content ID system.
- Even short or edited clips containing copyrighted video or audio may trigger automatic removal.
- Dubbed or translated scenes from commercial films still carry copyright and are frequently blocked.
- Content using soundtracks from movies or popular music without a license is commonly muted or taken down.

### 9. Illegal Activities & Regulated Goods
- Content promoting or facilitating illegal activities is prohibited: drug use, poaching, illegal weapons, human trafficking.
- Sale or promotion of regulated goods without proper safeguards is restricted: firearms, alcohol, tobacco, prescription drugs, financial services.
- Content that provides instructions for illegal activities is prohibited.

### 10. Sexual Content & Nudity
- Explicit sexual activity, pornographic content, and sexual acts are prohibited.
- Sexual solicitation or sexually suggestive content involving minors is strictly prohibited.
- Non-explicit content about sexual health, education, or awareness with appropriate context may be allowed.
- Nudity in artistic, educational, or documentary contexts with appropriate restrictions may be allowed.

### 11. Misinformation & Harmful Conspiracies
- Content that causes harm by spreading medical misinformation is prohibited.
- Content that disputes verified election outcomes is prohibited.
- Content promoting harmful conspiracy theories that incite violence or hatred is prohibited.
- Content that denies well-documented violent events is prohibited.
- Synthetic or manipulated media that misleads users about real events is subject to labeling or removal.

### 12. Integrity & Authenticity
- Synthetic or AI-generated content depicting real individuals saying or doing things they did not must be labeled.
- Impersonation of creators, brands, or public figures is prohibited.
- Misleading content that deceives users about its nature, origin, or purpose is prohibited.

## Recommendation Guidelines (For You Feed Eligibility)

### 13. Content Quality & Originality
- Videos should be well-produced with clear video and audio quality.
- Content that is blurry, poorly lit, or has distracting audio artifacts may be demoted in recommendations.
- Low-effort or template-based content lacking original creation is ineligible for recommendation.
- Content must represent original creative effort; simple compilations of others' work may be demoted.
- Videos should be appropriately formatted for vertical viewing (9:16 aspect ratio preferred).

### 14. Clickbait & Misleading Content
- Hooks that misrepresent the actual content of the video are prohibited from recommendation.
- Thumbnails or preview frames that are intentionally misleading are demoted.
- Titles and descriptions must accurately reflect the video content.
- Content that tricks users into watching through false promises is ineligible.
- "Wait for it" or similar engagement traps without payoff may be demoted.

### 15. Repetitive & Low-Value Content
- Content that is substantially similar to existing popular content without adding value may be demoted.
- Content farms producing high volumes of low-effort videos are restricted.
- Recycled content reposted from other platforms without original transformation is limited.
- Content with excessive watermarks from other platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.) is demoted.

### 16. Engagement Bait
- Content explicitly asking for likes, shares, comments, or follows to manipulate the algorithm is demoted.
- Content promising rewards for engagement (giveaways requiring engagement) must follow platform promotion rules.
- Contests or challenges designed solely to drive engagement without substantive content may be limited.
- "React for react" or similar reciprocal engagement schemes are prohibited.

### 17. Metadata & Hashtag Rules
- Hashtags must be relevant to the content. Unrelated trending hashtags to gain visibility are prohibited.
- Description must not contain spam or misleading links.
- Using excessive hashtags (>10) may be treated as spam and limit reach.
- Automated or bot-generated comments and descriptions are prohibited.

### 18. Platform Integrity
- Content encouraging users to leave TikTok or other platforms may be demoted.
- Links to harmful or misleading external content are prohibited.
- Cross-platform watermark removal or obfuscation is a violation.
- Content must comply with all applicable local laws and regulations.

### 19. Monetization & Branded Content
- Branded content and sponsorships must be disclosed according to local regulations.
- Content promoting gambling, dating services, or certain financial products must comply with age restrictions.
- Misleading claims about products or services in promotional content are prohibited.
- Affiliate links and promotional codes must follow platform commerce policies.

### 20. Duration & Format Recommendations
- Videos between 21-34 seconds perform best for recommendation distribution.
- Videos under 10 seconds are often too short for meaningful engagement.
- Videos over 60 seconds must maintain strong retention throughout.
- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio is strongly recommended for full-screen viewing.
- First 1-3 seconds must capture attention immediately.
- Videos should feel complete and not cut off abruptly.
