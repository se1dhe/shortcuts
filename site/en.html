Before you start

What do I need to run it?

An Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 15+, Xcode with Swift 6, XcodeGen, FFmpeg and your chosen local models. The README includes build instructions. Memory and disk requirements depend on the model and video.

What runs locally?

MLX runs Director, WhisperKit transcribes speech, and the media pipeline processes files on your Mac. Downloads and publishing use the network. Platform accounts require your own sign-in.

Can I change the model’s decisions?

Yes. Edit scenes, timing, order, text, subtitles, sound and packaging manually. AI output is validated, but editorial review is still essential.

Are films and music included?

No. Use your own materials with the appropriate rights. The app does not guarantee monetization or freedom from platform claims. Screenshots show the actual editor.
